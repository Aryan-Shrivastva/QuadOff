# Review thresholds

FieldNote uses project review bands of dissolved oxygen below 6.0 mg/L or
turbidity above 10 NTU to prioritize evidence. These are not regulatory
limits. Regulations vary by jurisdiction and designated use; consult the
current authority before making a report or decision.
