# River Zone 3 baseline

The Clearwater field team uses River Zone 3 as the reference reach for
seasonal water-quality checks. In the curated project pack, the normal range
for dissolved oxygen is 6.0–9.5 mg/L and the normal turbidity range is 0–10
NTU. Readings outside those ranges are evidence to review, not an automatic
contamination diagnosis.

The August 2026 samples show two low-oxygen observations and three high-
turbidity observations after rainfall. Compare a new observation with the
nearest upstream site and record weather, time, instrument ID, and calibration
status before escalating.
