# Watershed risk summary

Stormwater pulses can raise suspended sediment and lower dissolved oxygen for
several hours. A single reading should be treated as a lead. A repeated
pattern across a nearby site and two sampling rounds is stronger evidence.

The project team should preserve the original measurement and instrument
metadata. Never replace a field measurement with a model-generated value.
